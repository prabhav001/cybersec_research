DISTILLED PAIN POINTS
- Offensive AI measurement is dominated by provider-side disclosures; victim-side defenders lack validated telemetry signatures to tell agentic from human or scripted operators.
- Vendors disagree on how much AI changes outcomes (Mandiant's restraint vs CrowdStrike's +89% operations), so prevalence is poorly measured.

CAVEATS
- GTG-1002 percentages are Anthropic-reported figures relayed by press; treat 80-90% as "reported by the provider".
