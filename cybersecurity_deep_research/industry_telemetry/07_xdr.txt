1. [E01][P] Mandiant M-Trends 2026
   - Most targeted/abused: edge and core network devices (VPNs, routers) "typically lack standard endpoint detection and response (EDR) telemetry"; virtualization platforms; identity and backup systems; SaaS (OAuth tokens, session cookies); IT help desks via voice phishing.
   - "interactive social engineering frequently bypasses traditional MFA."
   - Attackers harvest "long-lived OAuth tokens and session cookies"; compromise SaaS vendors to steal hard-coded keys/PATs and pivot into downstream customers.
   URL: https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/m-trends-2026

DISTILLED PAIN POINTS
- The XDR value proposition (cross-domain correlation) depends on telemetry from planes where attackers actually operate (identity/token, edge, SaaS, hypervisor) - exactly the planes with weakest or absent sensors.
- Session/token theft breaks the identity<->endpoint link: the IdP sees a valid login, the endpoint sees nothing.

CAVEATS
- No independent quantification of cross-domain correlation quality was found. Vendor "XDR ceiling" papers are advocacy.
