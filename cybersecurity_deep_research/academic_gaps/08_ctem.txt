STRUCTURAL LIMITATIONS / FUTURE-WORK SCOPES
- Scalability of multi-cloud identity + network + vulnerability graphs is claimed by tools but not independently benchmarked under churn (ephemeral pods/functions).
- "Validated exposure" lacks a formal, calibrated definition; production-safety constraints on validation are unaddressed in surfaced literature.
- Predictive validity (do high-ranked paths match real incident chains?) is untested.
