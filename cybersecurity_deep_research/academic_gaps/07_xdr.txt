STRUCTURAL LIMITATIONS / FUTURE-WORK SCOPES
- Provenance IDS research targets single-host/endpoint audit logs; it does not model missing planes (edge, SaaS) or identity-to-host joins.
- Alert granularity (tens of thousands of nodes) defeats analyst consumption; attribution at node level is weak.
- Benchmarks are dated/short; ground truth for modern LOLBin/token-theft chains is scarce.
