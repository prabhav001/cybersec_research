   URL: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/google-warns-of-new-ai-powered-malware-families-deployed-in-the-wild/

DISTILLED PAIN POINTS
- Intelligence is consumed on human timescales (feed ingestion, ticket triage) while adversary hand-offs and breakouts occur in seconds-to-minutes.
- Indicator-centric CTI covers a shrinking slice of activity (malware-free, valid-account, LOLBin-heavy intrusions) and decays at actor-specific rates not captured by vendor TTLs.
- Adversaries are embedding LLM calls inside malware, making static signatures and fixed indicators less durable.

CAVEATS
