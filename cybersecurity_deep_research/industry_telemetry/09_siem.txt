DISTILLED PAIN POINTS
- Coverage is a detection-engineering throughput and validation problem, not a log-availability problem (21% coverage vs >90% theoretical).
- Rules silently die (13%): schema drift, parser changes, missing fields - and nobody notices because dead rules produce no alerts.
- High false-positive rates coexist with low true coverage: both are symptoms of unvalidated rules.

CAVEATS
- CardinalOps and SANS are vendor-relayed. CardinalOps' "coverage" is mapping-based (rules tagged to techniques), not execution-validated.
