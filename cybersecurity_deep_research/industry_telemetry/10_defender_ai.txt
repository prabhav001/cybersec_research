4. [E01][P] M-Trends 2026: QUIETVAULT probes for local AI CLI tools; distillation attacks target proprietary ML models.
5. [E03][S] Microsoft DDR 2025 (secondary): recommends AI for large-scale log analysis and anomaly detection; discusses non-human identities (service accounts, AI agents) as an emerging identity class.

DISTILLED PAIN POINTS
- Defender LLMs ingest attacker-controlled text (user-agents, URLs, DNS names, command lines, email bodies) as part of normal operation; the trust boundary between instructions and data does not exist in the model.
- Defenders are deploying agents with action capabilities (ticketing, containment, rule edits) while adversaries have begun embedding payloads aimed at those agents.

CAVEATS
