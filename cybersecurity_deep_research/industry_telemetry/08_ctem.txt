DISTILLED PAIN POINTS
- The exposures that matter in practice are configuration/identity chains (AD CS, OAuth, IAM trust, backup permissions), not CVE counts; scanner-centric prioritization misses them.
- Validation (the 4th CTEM stage) is the scarcest capability: safe-in-production emulation and its fidelity are rarely quantified.
- The headline efficacy claim for CTEM lacks independent empirical validation.

CAVEATS
- Nearly all CTEM adoption/efficacy statistics are vendor-relayed; none were verified against Gartner directly.
